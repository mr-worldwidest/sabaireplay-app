Privacy.

Last updated September 18, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Sabai Replay handles information when you use the app and this website.

Information you provide

Sabai Replay may process the videos, round details, and settings you choose to add to the app. This content is used to provide the app’s replay and review features.

Device permissions

The app may request access to your camera, microphone, or photo library when a feature needs it. You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate, maintain, and improve Sabai Replay, respond to support requests, and protect the app from misuse.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when necessary to operate Sabai Replay, or when required by law.

Retention and your choices

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described above. You can remove content in the app or contact support to ask about access or deletion.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Sabai Replay evolves. The date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@sabaireplay.app.
