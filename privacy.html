Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026
Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sabai Replay (“Sabai Replay,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and stores information when you use the Sabai Replay mobile application (the “App”) and this website (together, the “Services”).

By using the Services, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

Summary: Sabai Replay is designed so your training videos and round content stay on your device. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your content for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

1. Who we are

Sabai Replay is a mobile training replay app that helps you capture, review, and improve performance round by round.

Privacy questions and requests: support@sabaireplay.app

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to operate Sabai Replay and respond to you. This Privacy Policy identifies what data we collect, how we collect it, and how we use it.

2.1 Information you create in the App (on-device)

When you use Sabai Replay, the App may process and store on your device:

  • Videos and photos you record or import
  • Audio captured with your recordings, if microphone access is enabled
  • Round details, timers, notes, tags, and other training metadata you enter
  • App preferences and settings you choose

This content is stored locally on your device for the App’s replay and review features. We do not upload your videos, photos, audio, or round content to our servers as part of normal App use.

2.2 Information you provide when contacting us

If you email support or otherwise contact us, we collect the information you choose to send, which may include your email address, device model, iOS version, App version, and the details of your request.

2.3 Device permissions

Depending on the features you use, the App may request permission to access:

  • Camera — to record training video
  • Microphone — to capture audio with recordings
  • Photo Library — to import or save media you choose

These permissions are requested only when needed for the related feature. You can deny or withdraw permission at any time in your device Settings. Denying a permission may limit related features, but paid functionality is not conditioned on granting access to unnecessary data.

2.4 Information we do not collect

Unless this policy is updated and you are notified, Sabai Replay does not:

  • Require you to create an account
  • Collect precise location
  • Collect contacts, calendar, or health data
  • Use advertising SDKs or sell advertising based on your data
  • Track you across apps or websites owned by other companies
  • Share your personal data with third-party AI providers

3. How we collect information

We collect information in these ways:

  • Directly from you — when you record or import media, enter round details, change settings, or contact support
  • Through device permissions you grant — camera, microphone, and photo library access as described above
  • Automatically on your device — App preferences and content you create are stored locally by the App

4. How we use information

We use information solely to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain Sabai Replay’s recording, replay, and review features
  • Remember your settings and improve App reliability and performance on your device
  • Respond to support requests and communicate with you about your inquiry
  • Protect against misuse, abuse, or technical issues
  • Comply with applicable law and enforce our legal rights

We do not use your training videos or personal information for advertising, profiling for ads, or selling data.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing.

We may share limited information only in these cases:

  • Service providers — for example, if you email us, your message is processed by our email/support tools solely to respond to you. Those providers are permitted to use the information only to provide services to us and are expected to protect it consistently with this policy.
  • Legal requirements — when required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, us, or others.
  • Business transfers — if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy or a successor policy with equal or greater protections.
  • With your direction — if you choose to export or share content yourself using iOS share sheets or other tools you control.

Apple Inc. and your device operating system may process certain technical information under their own privacy policies when you download the App from the App Store or use iOS features. That processing is governed by Apple’s policies, not ours.

6. Tracking and advertising

Sabai Replay does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework for cross-app tracking, and does not display third-party advertising in the App.

7. Data retention

On-device content (videos, photos, audio, round details, and settings) remains on your device until you delete it in the App, remove the App, or otherwise clear it using device controls.

Support communications are retained only as long as needed to resolve your request and for legitimate business, security, or legal purposes, then deleted or de-identified.

8. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • Delete videos, photos, and round content in the App
  • Change or revoke camera, microphone, and photo library permissions in iOS Settings
  • Uninstall the App to remove locally stored App data from your device (subject to iOS behavior)
  • Contact us to withdraw consent for any processing that relies on your consent, or to request access to or deletion of personal information we hold about you (such as support emails)

To make a privacy request, email support@sabaireplay.app with the subject line “Privacy Request.” We will respond within a reasonable period and as required by applicable law.

Because Sabai Replay does not require an account, there is no separate account to delete. Deleting App content on your device and requesting deletion of any support records we hold is the way to remove your information from the Services.

9. Children’s privacy

Sabai Replay is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact support@sabaireplay.app and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information we receive (such as support emails). On-device content is protected by your device’s security features. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International users

The Services are operated from the United States. If you use the Services from another country, any information you send us (for example, by email) may be processed in the United States or other locations where we or our service providers operate.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. If changes are material, we will provide additional notice as required by law (for example, in the App or on this page). Continued use of the Services after an update means you accept the revised policy.
